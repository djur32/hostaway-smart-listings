	**In one line:** every listing detail lives once, in Shared, and each channel holds only what differs. That became a channel-first property menu, chosen in design review and not yet tested with hosts.

## What came out of it
- **One object model.** Listing details live once, in the Listing base. The Knowledge Hub reads the listing and keeps no copy of it, and adds what the listing doesn't say: the situations behind a rule, and guests' questions. Airbnb, Booking.com and Direct hold only what differs.
- **Four cases for every channel field:** it uses the Base, it is custom for that channel, it exists only on that channel (Airbnb's check-in method), or it is set by the channel and the host can't change it (Booking.com writes its own description).
- **Record-first, then channel-first.** The IA work first recommended a record-first menu. When Smart Listings needed a home, five navigation models were drawn, and I chose channel-first: Shared first, then one group per channel.
- **The main risk was designed out.** Shared values have one home, and a channel page speaks only about its own channel.
- **Built, not tested.** The sitemap below is the live prototype. The tree test that would check it is planned.
