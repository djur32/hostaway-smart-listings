## The solution in one paragraph
Listing details live once, in the **Listing base** (the Details, Pricing and Availability every channel lists). The **Knowledge Hub** reads those details without copying them, adds what they don't say (the situations behind a rule, such as what a host does when X, Y or Z happens), and learns from guests' questions in the inbox. Each **channel** (Airbnb, Booking.com, Direct) has its own pages, where a field **uses the Base**, is **customised for that channel**, exists **only on that channel** (Airbnb's check-in method), or is **set by the channel** and can't be changed (Booking.com writes its own description). **Smart Listings** is opt-in: a **strategy per channel** turns into **tips** on that channel's pages, each with its *why* and its sources. It isn't a new AI: it is Hospitable's own **AI Copilot**, expanded from answering questions to working on the listing, with the same sparkle and the same purple. The host accepts or dismisses them, previews the result on a **Canvas** of the real guest page, and checks in **Performance** whether the strategy worked. ## The story in six steps
1. **Today the Knowledge Hub repeats the listing and has no job of its own.** Its copy drifts and Copilot answers guests from it. No screen shows one listing on one channel, and "Edit per channel" is scattered across the forms. `REAL·PUBLIC` · `REAL·PRODUCT` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af16458127800cde5c8b1017c8">01 · Discovery</mention-page>
2. **Each channel plays by its own rules.** Limits differ, and Booking.com writes its own description. `REAL·PUBLIC` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645818c859bcd3115fce28e">Channel constraints</mention-page>
3. **Hosts vary money and stay rules per channel far more than words.** So tips must cover pricing, availability and policies, and a channel's own voice is a choice, not a default. `SIMULATED` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581e3a9faf5f1f8e138f3">02 · Research</mention-page>
4. **Facts once; each channel holds only what differs.** A channel-first menu: Shared first, then one group per channel. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581329bede8618280f79b">03 · Information architecture</mention-page>
5. **A strategy per channel turns into tips with their why,** in context, decided by the host. The tips are AI Copilot reaching into the listing, not a second assistant to learn. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581b690aecaa695cf08a7">04 · Concepts</mention-page> · <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645810bb3b3c9d7bf2ab924">05 · Design</mention-page>
6. **Next: test it with hosts, measure it, then import and optimisation over time.** `PROJECTED` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645813d9675c3b09fcf3546">06 · Test & measure</mention-page> · <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645813ea2e7d6c6dbf87ddf">07 · Systems & next</mention-page>
