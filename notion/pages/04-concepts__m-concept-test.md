## Why this method
The direction was chosen in design review. A concept test is where that judgement gets challenged. Its job is not to pick a winner but to find where hosts behave differently from what I assumed, above all over-trust: the friendlier the surface, the less of it gets read.
## How I'd run it
<table header-row="false" header-column="true">
	<tr>
		<td>Who</td>

	</tr>
	<tr>

## What I'd look for
- **Over-trust in the Canvas.** Do new hosts approve a friendly preview without reading it, and miss a money change there?
- **Is the why read before Accept?** Can a host say, in their own words, why a tip was suggested?
- **Do hosts feel the strategy as theirs?** Does the wizard's goal and audience explain the tips they then see?
- **Per-channel voice as a choice.** Who wants a channel's own text, and who wants one text everywhere?
