	**In one line:** how I'd find out whether Smart Listings works before trusting it: a test programme, a north star with guardrails, and a pre-mortem. All of it is planned; none of it has been run.
</callout>
## What came out of it
- A programme, not a list of studies: benchmark today's product first, then two think-aloud rounds, a tree test, a structure A/B, a pilot with a private Slack channel, and a rollout with a holdout.
- Eight tasks hosts would do in the prototype, from a Booking.com cleaning fee to a tip's why, with probes like "Where does this fact live? What happens on Airbnb if you change it here?"
- **North star: time to adjust.** Trust is read next to it: tip acceptance beside AI copy reverted, satisfaction and perceived control.
- **It drives five goals**, each with a target at 30 and 90 days: bookings and booking requests up 5% and 7% against each listing's own weeks before, 10% and 25% of eligible properties adopt it, 60% and 50% of adopters keep refining, 30% and 40% of tips are accepted, and listing and sync tickets fall 5% and 10%. Each goal names the riskiest assumption it tests (trust in AI tips, a voice per channel, what Autopilot may touch) and the study that checks it.
- Four guardrails: no unsupported AI claims, undo rate, wrong-channel edits, channel rejections.
- Seven ways it could fail, each with an early signal, the test that catches it, and what I'd do.
