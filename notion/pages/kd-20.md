# KD-20 · Smart Listings expands AI Copilot. It isn't a new AI
Area: Tips & AI · Stage: Concepts · Status: Decided · Signal: Moderate · Provenance: REAL·PUBLIC, REAL·PRODUCT, ASSUMPTION
Records: D-020, D-907, D-939, D-949, D-968
URL: https://app.notion.com/p/3efe18af164581ceabafdfe5275589dd

**Decision.** Smart Listings doesn't introduce a new assistant. It expands Hospitable's existing **AI Copilot**, which today answers hosts' business questions and writes replies, so that it also works on the listing: tips on each channel's pages, Rewrite with AI on a field, and Listing base facts and guest questions in the Knowledge Hub as its sources. It keeps Copilot's sparkle icon and its purple on lavender.
**Why**
- Hosts already know Copilot. Building on it means no new name, icon or place to learn, and the trust it has earned carries over (`REAL·PUBLIC`: [hospitable.com](https://hospitable.com) presents AI Copilot with a sparkle, purple on lavender).
- The product already speaks AI in this language: the same sparkle sits in the sidebar footer, and Dynamic Pricing and Ask the AI use the lavender (`REAL·PRODUCT`).
- One assistant that grows across the product is a synergy, not another feature to sell.
**Options considered**
- A separate listing AI with its own name and look: dropped, a second assistant to learn.
- A chat copilot on every screen: dropped as the main surface; its job became the docked Tips panel and Rewrite with AI.
- **Expand AI Copilot into the listing: chosen.**
