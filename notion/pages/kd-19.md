**Decision.** Judge Smart Listings by **time to adjust**: how long a host takes to make a real change correctly, timed in the lab against today's product and in the product against a holdout. Trust is read next to it: tip acceptance beside AI copy reverted, satisfaction and perceived control. Four guardrails: no unsupported AI claims, undo rate, wrong-channel edits, channel rejections.
**Why**
- It's the model's promise in one number: facts once, channels hold only what differs, tips in context. It's also the measure I named.
- It counts for every host, including those who keep Smart Listings off. Tip acceptance alone can rise while copy gets worse.
- It's measured twice, so a lab win that doesn't hold in real use shows up.
**Options considered**
- Tip acceptance, Smart Listings opt-in, undo rate or satisfaction as the north star: kept as trust measures and guardrails.
- Confirmed-live rate in 7 days, the original north star: it measures import, so it leads the import release on the Roadmap.
- Time to adjust, with trust read beside it: chosen.
