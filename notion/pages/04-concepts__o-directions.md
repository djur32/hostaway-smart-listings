## The short version
- **Round one:** seven directions, each with its own mental model, primary object and first action, through the same five screens in Hostaway's design system. The bar: if two are the same screen with the panel on the other side, one of them is wrong.
- **Round two:** twelve AI-guided Smart Listing directions in Paper, each built on established patterns from best-in-class products and run through the same flow: Overview → listing text → a missing fact → Pricing → Availability → Booking policies → review → receipts.
- Every direction kept the same hard rules: nothing reaches a channel unseen, money and stay rules never go on autopilot, an unknown fact becomes a question, and the real channel limits apply.
- The live canvas and the copilot were recommended; strategy pieces came from two others. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581bdb0f8dd25c3d311a4">Final concept</mention-page>

## How it was made
Round one: one-page briefs and mid-fi frames per direction, a written critique of each (persona fit, where it breaks), and a weighted evaluation against the principles (). Round two: each direction is a pattern board with its saved references plus eight frames, built in Paper on a Hostaway replica, with two compare boards at the end.
