## What came out of it
- Shared becomes the record the whole product reads, so a listing fact also reaches guest replies, pricing, operations and owner statements.
- The honest cost is blast radius: one wrong fact now reaches every channel and every guest reply. So every fact shows its source and its reach.
- Channel terms and the comps dataset need Integrations and Legal on day one, not at launch.
- **Now:** test and harden Smart Listings. **Next:** import and first-time setup (Angle A) on the same model, with its own north star. **Later:** optimisation over time (Angle C), without bringing versions back.
- Four lines of work the build implies but doesn't finish: similar homes per channel, a deeper Knowledge Hub, Performance done thoroughly, and tips any product area can raise through one contract. Each has its owners and the measure that says it worked.
- Five kill criteria, written down before any result.
