## What came out of it
- **Seven design principles,** written before any direction and edited by the research. Three are floors every direction had to meet.
- **Round one:** seven mental models through the same screens. The Decision Inbox and the Channel Canvas were the finalists, as a pair.
- **Round two:** twelve AI-guided Smart Listing directions in Paper, each built on established patterns from best-in-class products. The live canvas and the copilot were recommended.
- **My reviews** merged them into one Smart Listing model and moved it into the prototype, with the channel-first menu.
- **The copilot became the assistant, expanded.** Instead of a new chat, the copilot direction became Hostaway's existing the assistant working on the listing, with the same sparkle and colours. <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3efe18af164581ceabafdfe5275589dd">KD-20</mention-page>
- **Not tested with hosts.** A concept test is planned; nothing here is host evidence.
