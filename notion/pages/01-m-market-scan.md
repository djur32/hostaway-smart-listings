## Why this method
Smart Listings writes to channels Hostaway doesn't control. Before designing per-channel pages and AI tips, I needed each channel's real rules, the patterns others already use for "one record, many outputs", and evidence of what hosts ask for. So I started from the channels' public docs.

## What I looked for
- What each channel lets a PMS write, and who authors the guest-facing text.
- How others show "inherited vs changed for this channel".
- How AI suggestions earn trust: grounding, provenance, accept or dismiss.
- What hosts ask for: edit once, differ on purpose, or both.
## What it found that changed the plan
- **Booking.com writes its own description.** That refuted an assumption before research began, and made Booking.com's description read-only in the design. See <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645819282b2f11c14720c03">Research questions & assumptions</mention-page>.
- **Hosts want both.** On the board, "Ability to update all listing content from Hostaway" has 322 votes and is In Progress. And 25 posts ask for per-channel differences on purpose (minimum stay, markups, check-in times, discounts, photos, fees). [feedback.hostaway.com](https://feedback.hostaway.com/p/two-way-sync-of-updates-to-listing-details)
- **Almost nobody asks for AI listing copy in public**, and the few AI voices object to channels rewriting text without consent. So demand for AI copy is an assumption to test, and the design leads with consent and sources.
