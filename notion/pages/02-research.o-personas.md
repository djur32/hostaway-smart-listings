## The short version
- Four hosts, four meanings of "listing management": it just works, every word tuned, change once for many, and "why would Hostaway touch it at all?".
- They differ most on how much the AI may do. Family and new hosts over-trust it; the portfolio manager trusts rules, not cards.
- So Smart Listings changes defaults and guidance per host, never what the product can do.
<columns>
	<column>
		### Marta · family host
		**One flat, on Airbnb and Booking.com.** Hosting isn't the main income.
		- **Listing management means:** it just works, and only differences need attention.
		- **Fears:** a silent money error, like a cleaning fee that shows €0 on Booking.com and turns up at payout; losing reviews.
		- **Trust in AI:** accepts an explained tip more readily, but won't check it. "Don't make me the checker."
		- **What Smart Listings does:** off by default. One text for every channel; prices, fees and rules never change without asking.
	</column>
	<column>
		### Dana · SEO optimiser
		**Five units on four channels.** Edits copy every week and enjoys it.
		- **Listing management means:** tuning the title, opening and photo order to each channel's guests.
		- **Fears:** a weekend's rewrite overwritten, or an edit that silently never arrives.
		- **Trust in AI:** a first draft to decide on. Wants to see the similar homes behind any claim.
		- **What Smart Listings does:** on by default, with per-channel voice, tips, Canvas and Performance open.
	</column>
</columns>
<columns>
	<column>
		### Callum · portfolio manager
		**42 units, 27 owners, a team of four.** Came from another PMS over sync trouble.
		- **Listing management means:** change once for many units, see the exceptions first, show owners what changed.
		- **Fears:** restrictions, minimum stays and rate plans wiped in the first hour; a VA accepting a bad claim at volume.
		- **Trust in AI:** low per item, high for rules set once. "I'd want rules, not cards."
		- **What Smart Listings does:** on by default; Autopilot with review inside portfolio rules; one text adapted per channel.
	</column>
	<column>
		### Priya · new to a PMS
		**Two units, Airbnb only.** Joined for automated messages, not for listings.
		- **Listing management means:** nothing yet. "Why would I? It's on Airbnb."
		- **Fears:** losing reviews; changes nobody asked for.
		- **Trust in AI:** high and uncritical. Missed the invented claim twice; over-trust is the risk.
		- **What Smart Listings does:** off by default; fewer, safer tips; facts never edited by AI; more explanations.
	</column>
</columns>

**The churned host (a lens).** Rob left on day 52 because "I couldn't tell what it was doing". That is what any of the four becomes when the product's status is invisible.
## In the prototype
- The account menu at the foot of the sidebar has **Act as**: Dana (the default), Callum, Marta and Priya. Each account keeps its own saved work.
- An account changes defaults and guidance, never capability. **Settings › How Hostaway helps you** shows the preset (for example "One home · Standard guidance" for Marta), the sign-up answers that chose it, and every default, each one changeable.
- Smart Listings starts off for Marta and Priya, and on for Dana and Callum. Priya already has her one listing, so she starts on it like everyone else. There is no first-time setup in the prototype.
The accounts share the demo's Berlin homes rather than the research's cities and portfolio sizes, and there is no Vrbo. The research had Dana keep the main channel to drafts; the demo runs Autopilot on Dana's Airbnb (text and photo order only) to show the mode. The prototype wins. {color="gray"}
