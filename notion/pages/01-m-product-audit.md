## Why this method
Smart Listings changes how a host edits one listing per channel. Before changing that, I needed the real map: where listing data lives, how "different per channel" works, what the AI already does, and where editing breaks down. The supplied screenshots and a free trial made this possible. ## What I did

Then four passes over the material:
1. **IA map:** the property menu as built, where navigation leaves the property, and where the same data appears twice.
2. **Field inventory:** every listing field, its control, its "ⓘ N channels" label, whether it can differ per channel, and its implied sync direction. 135 fields.
3. **Knowledge Hub ledger:** each fact in Details compared with the Hub's copy on a trial listing, to see how the Hub repeats the listing. 34 facts.
4. **Heuristic review:** Nielsen's 10 plus six listing heuristics, severity 0–4, one evaluator. 26 findings on listing management, each tagged with the heuristic it breaks.
## What I looked for
- Where a listing's facts live, and whether the copies agree.
- How a host makes one thing different on one channel.
- What the AI does today, and with which data.
- Where editing, saving and moving around break down.
