## What came out of it
- **A working prototype**, as a website and a phone app, of Smart Listings on the Listing base & channels model. It is the source of truth for every page in this section.
- **Coded on Hospitable's real design tokens**, replicating its screens one by one and departing only where the model needs it.
- **Its AI is AI Copilot, expanded.** Tips and Rewrite are Hospitable's existing AI Copilot working on the listing, with Copilot's sparkle and purple on lavender, so nothing about the AI is new to learn. <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3efe18af164581ceabafdfe5275589dd">KD-20</mention-page>
- **Shaped through my own design reviews.** Each round changed the build itself. Versions, a compare mode, a Setup page and inline editing on the Canvas were built and then removed.
- **Built in code rather than static mockups**, so every state can be clicked and every claim here can be checked in the prototype.
- **Not tested with hosts yet.** <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645813d9675c3b09fcf3546">06 · Test & measure</mention-page> plans how.
