## The solution in one paragraph
Every fact about the home lives once, in **Shared**: the **Listing base** (details, pricing and availability that every channel lists) and the **Knowledge Hub** (what the guest assistant knows). Each **channel** (Airbnb, Booking⁠.com, Direct) has its own pages, where a field **uses the Base**, is **customised for that channel**, or is **set by the channel** (Booking⁠.com writes its own description). **Smart Listings** is opt-in: a **strategy per channel** turns into **tips** on that channel's pages, each with its *why* and its sources. The host accepts or dismisses them, previews the result on a **Canvas** of the real guest page, and checks in **Performance** whether the strategy worked. Start with <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581828521eb27e3e50737"/>, or open the prototype: [website](https://hospitable-case-djur32.vercel.app) · [phone app](https://hospitable-case-mobile-djur32.vercel.app).
## The story in six steps
1. **Today the facts live twice.** Details and the Knowledge Hub disagree, no screen shows one listing on one channel, and "Edit per channel" is scattered across the forms. `REAL·PRODUCT` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af16458127800cde5c8b1017c8">01 · Discovery</mention-page>
2. **Each channel plays by its own rules.** Limits differ, and Booking⁠.com writes its own description. `REAL·PUBLIC` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645818c859bcd3115fce28e"/>
3. **Hosts vary money and stay rules per channel far more than words.** So tips must cover pricing, availability and policies, and a channel's own voice is a choice, not a default. `SIMULATED` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581e3a9faf5f1f8e138f3">02 · Research</mention-page>
4. **Facts once; each channel holds only what differs.** A channel-first menu: Shared first, then one group per channel. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581329bede8618280f79b">03 · Information architecture</mention-page>
5. **A strategy per channel turns into tips with their why,** in context, decided by the host. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581b690aecaa695cf08a7">04 · Concepts</mention-page> · <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645810bb3b3c9d7bf2ab924">05 · Design</mention-page>
6. **Next: test it with hosts, measure it, then import and optimisation over time.** `PROJECTED` → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645813d9675c3b09fcf3546">06 · Test & measure</mention-page> · <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645813ea2e7d6c6dbf87ddf">07 · Systems & next</mention-page>
