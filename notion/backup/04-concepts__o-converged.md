## The short version
- **Chosen:** B2's live canvas and B3's copilot, merged into one Smart Listing model, with the strategy taken from B6 (who it's for) and B7 (goal and guardrails).
- **Why:** the canvas answers the brief's control-and-transparency question most directly, because the host edits what guests will see on a faithful copy of each channel's page. The copilot keeps less confident hosts on screens they already know. The strategy gives every tip a reason beyond a single rule.
- **My review of B2** (43 comments in Paper) set the model: switched on per property, a strategy per channel, one tip container in a side panel, a Classic and a Canvas view, and performance with basic numbers.
- **Then it left Paper** for the coded prototype, with the channel-first menu.
- **Three things were dropped on the way**, and each made the product smaller.
## From two finalists to one model
1. **Round one** paired the Decision Inbox (the spine: only what needs you, money first) with the Channel Canvas (the surface: the channel's real page). Its "one home" became Shared, its "window per channel" became the channel pages, and "what needs you" became tips.
2. **Round two** recommended the live canvas with the copilot as its suggestion layer. The five later directions added parts: the tip's diff and why, and similar homes as evidence.
3. **My review of B2** merged them into one model: a strategy per channel from B6's "who it's for" and B7's goal and guardrails, Autopilot or tips only, Classic and Canvas, and Performance.
4. **The next review** found the four-step strategy too complex and preferred the simplicity of B7's first screens, so the strategy became a short wizard: one question per screen, on Hospitable's own get-started pattern.
5. **A later review** moved the work off Paper: "Implement the whole thing now. Don't do it on Paper, do it on the real thing." The prototype was built with the channel-first menu. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af16458180a645fc510d470034"/>

## What was dropped on the way
- **The listing studio inside the shared record.** Text is edited on each channel's Listing page. The title panel, the sentence toolbar and the similar-listings block gave way to one AI affordance per field: a tip, otherwise Rewrite with AI.
- **Versions and compare.** Performance compares each channel with the same stretch before its strategy started, and receipts, plus Undo on an accepted tip, are the safety net.
- **Setup as a separate page.** It was folded into the full-screen strategy wizard, which also edits and replaces strategies.
- Also not built: the copilot chat from the Paper canvas, and the three-level autonomy dial, now Autopilot on or off.
## What it became
Smart Listings is opt-in per property. A strategy per channel, made in a full-screen wizard, turns into tips on that channel's pages, each with its why and sources. The host accepts or dismisses them, sees the result on the Canvas as guests would, and checks in Performance whether the strategy worked. The whole build, screen by screen: <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af164581828521eb27e3e50737"/>.
## How it was made
Each of my comments was mapped to its frame and an owner. Four Paper workstreams (strategy and performance, Canvas, Classic, navigation) rebuilt the model, then the prototype replaced Paper and every later review happened on running code. No host has tested the direction. → <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3eee18af1645810fbe60d21eb29dd3c7"/>
