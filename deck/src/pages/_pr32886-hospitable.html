The line I'd never use: “AI does everything.”

“Listing management done well is a competitive advantage” · BRIEF

Hospitable’s own brief says listing management done well is a competitive advantage, and AI makes that real. So the story I’d hand Marketing is simple: your listings, handled, and you still have the last word. Each claim is a screen I built. Your facts live once, and every field says whether it uses the Base, is custom for one channel, exists only on one, like Airbnb’s check-in method, or is set by the channel, like the description Booking.com writes, shown behind a lock that says why. Every tip is written for that channel’s strategy and carries its reason. You choose how much runs on its own, but money and stay rules always ask, even on Autopilot, and every change gets a receipt. I’d promise nothing the product doesn’t do, so the line I’d never use is AI does everything. The name is Marketing’s call. About 45 seconds.

Here is the order I’d go in, all on the same Base. Now: test and harden what’s built. Run the programme with hosts, agree with Integrations a confirmation for every field on every channel, so time to adjust can be measured in the product, and check the AI offline before any pilot. Next: import and first-time setup, so a new host sets up once, with its own north star: every channel confirmed live within seven days. Later: optimisation over time, on strategy periods rather than versions, with Autopilot widening only where tests show it helps.

Shared becomes the record the whole product reads, so these six patterns travel to guest replies, pricing, operations and owner statements. The honest cost is blast radius: one wrong fact reaches everywhere. That, and the five criteria that would make me stop, are in the detail. About 50 seconds.
