IntroScope and goals

The scope is an intelligent listing. We took it deeper.

We started from two questions. What does a listing look like when each channel can be different? And how much control do hosts need? Then we went deeper, in four ways. [Press] First, from copy to strategy. Every channel gets its own view and its own goal, not just new words. Second, from the text to the whole listing. The tips cover pricing, availability and booking policies too. They’re built on data: guests’ questions, the facts about the home, and similar homes. Third, and this matters most, you choose how much freedom it has. You can review every tip yourself. Or you can switch on an optional Autopilot that works inside your guardrails. You decide how much you stay in the loop. Fourth, from launch to results. Every strategy is tracked over time, against the weeks before it and week by week as it changes. So hosts keep what works. The alternative was a smarter text box on each channel. It answers the first question but not how much control hosts need, so we went wider. About 40 seconds.

IntroProcessWith AI

Two parts. First, the process we follow. These are the six stages, with the methods under each. Discovery is a product audit and heuristic evaluation, desk research on channels and competitors, assumption mapping and stakeholder interviews. Research is host interviews, data analysis and tree testing. Concept is seven directions on a weighted matrix, plus concept testing. Design is the prototype in code, reviewed round after round, the design system and an accessibility review. Testing is a benchmark test, usability tests and A/B tests, each defined before it runs. Systems is mapping, second-order effects, rollout and pilot design, and kill criteria. We chose stages over one big design sprint because each stage retires a risk before the next spends effort. One of our skills does the reading or the making. Then there’s a gate where we decide. Which hypotheses matter. Whether it’s a pattern or a loud few. Which direction. Whether the system holds.
