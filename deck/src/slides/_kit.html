Flow diagram · kit/flowmap.js

A flow, drawn live from JSON

Cards, decisions and plates from data/flows.json. Lines draw and cards pop in as a ladder.

figure.hc-flow with data-flow naming a diagram in data/flows.json, and data-variant="notion" draws the document variant (deck is the default). Node kinds: start, step, screen (with a glyph), ai, decision (who: host is raspberry), end, root. Plates are groups with a tint. "only": "deck" or "notion" keeps a node or a branch to one variant. The renderer sets data-a="flow", and data-still turns the motion off. Notion PNGs: node qa/diagrams/render.mjs.
