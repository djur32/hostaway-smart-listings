# illustrations/ · Hostaway mascot scenes (v4b, 3 Oct, fourth pass)

Generated by `ds/tools/illustrations.py` (one mascot construction shared by every scene, so they stay consistent). **Edit the script, not the SVGs**, then run `python3 ds/tools/illustrations.py` and `cd deck && node tools/sync-ds.mjs` (which also rewrites `deck/src/assets/illustrations/index.json`). `sheet.png` is the contact sheet of all nine at rest.

History: v2 put the mascot in the bright Hostaway raspberry, animated "ever so slightly". v3 gave every scene its own abstract head and an oversized figure. v4 (3 Oct) answered "way too much information, it's massive. Make it smaller and minimalistic… and conceptual": one idea per scene, the research scene redone, the earn scene without calendar or coins. **v4b (3 Oct) answers review's review of v4 on the covers**: "a little too empty: add a little more detail, not a lot, and maybe some decorative elements"; Systems & next's "document and arrow are very off brand… something better designed and more conceptual"; "I don't like that all of the people are standing on a line"; and on Design, "the arm we can see from the left is cut off" and "the head is a bit childish".

**The rules**, after Hostaway's own site (`research/references/hostaway-marketing/08` laptop mascot, `10` trumpet mascot, `11` "We believe" doodles):
- **One idea per scene**: the mascot plus ONE prop or symbol that carries it. No clusters of cards, notes, coins or calendars.
- **A little supporting detail** (a window and a chimney, a spark between two values, a channel dot per line, a small mark per tile) and **one or two decorative touches**: a soft rounded blob behind the prop (lavender `secondary-100`, or blush `primary-100` where the deck's cover is lavender), pale yellow "///" marker strokes (the deck's covers recolour them, `kit/hc.js`), a small lavender sparkle, at most one hand-drawn ring.
- **No ground line.** The figures stand free on a very soft contact shadow (aubergine at 7 %).
- **Smaller than v3, with air**: `Fig` k = 1.0 (v3 1.32; 1.3 on the 320 × 320 hero), about two thirds of the frame's height. No limb is cropped by the view box (checked: all content sits ≥ 10 px inside it), and no arm leaves the body where its contour is clipped.
- Transparent: no plate or panel baked in.

**Mascot**: a broad rounded torso, two thick tube legs with little feet, thick tube arms, mitten fists with three aubergine finger marks (or an open palm), and a small floating abstract head: a raspberry blob with one quirk and at most one small aubergine mark, different in every scene (`HEADS` in the script). Aubergine contours only where a limb crosses the body (clipped to the torso), on finger marks and on the odd object outline (tiles).

**Animation**: a `<style>` block inside each SVG, so it runs inlined or as `<img>` (checked in headless Chrome at rest, half way and at the full extent of every animation). A few named groups per file: `.up` (upper body bob, 2 px, 4.2 s; legs stay planted), `.head`, one prop or hand (`.arm`, `.drift`, `.d0–2`, `.ma`/`.mb`, `.pop`, `.drops`, `.bud`, `.pick`, `.waves`, `.dots`, `.b2`), and the decoration (`.spark`, `.doodle`, `.doodle2`, `.marks`, `.flow`, `.clash`). Periods 1.4–6.6 s, ease-in-out, alternate (the dotted trail is a linear loop). Keyframes only declare `to`, so frame 0 is the finished composition. Animated groups never carry a `transform` attribute (the scaled drawing sits in an inner `<g transform>`), and every pivot is in view-box px (`transform-box: view-box`) or the element's own box (`fill-box`). Selectors and keyframe names are prefixed per file (`.lt`, `.of`, `.cr`, `.aw`, `.nl`, `.rl`, `.cd`, `.tm`, `.ea`) and title/desc/clip ids are unique, so several can be inlined on one page. `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` sets `animation: none` on the SVG and everything in it (checked: 0 running animations with reduced motion forced).

**`mascot-earn.svg` parts, for a slide to animate in sequence**: the part groups carry no transform and no animation, and everything inside them is in view-box px of the 320×320 frame, so a slide can translate, scale, rotate or fade them freely; the idle motion runs on inner `ei-*` groups (and on the bars' own `<rect>`s) and keeps going underneath. Dropped in v4: `.earn-cal`, `.earn-checks`/`.earn-check-N`, `.earn-coins`/`.earn-coin-N`, `.earn-arrow`.
