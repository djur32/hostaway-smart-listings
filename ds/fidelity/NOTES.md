## Verdict

Shell, field row, tabs, buttons, AI card, radio cards, table and modal read as the same product at a glance; positions agree within ~2–4 CSS px in the measured areas. Colour matches Hostaway's own tokens at ΔE ≤ 0.6 for every role that was sampled (`ds/tools/samples.json`).
